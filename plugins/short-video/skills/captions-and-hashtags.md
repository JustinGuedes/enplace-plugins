# When to use
A piece is in Edit or Ready and needs its words for each channel.

# Caption
1. First line: a reason to watch to the end or to comment, in under 10 words. It shows before "more".
2. Second line, if needed: what it is and where to get it.
3. No hashtags inside sentences. No emoji strings.

# Hashtags
- Three to five: one broad (#mobilegames), two about the thing (#partygame, #boardgamenight), one about the moment (#gamenight).
- Never more than five, and never ones that don't match the video.

# Per channel
- **TikTok:** caption under 150 characters; the sound matters more than the text.
- **Instagram Reels:** the same caption works; add a line for the link in bio.
- **YouTube Shorts:** the title is the caption's first line, under 60 characters; put the link in the description.

# Subtitles
Add burned-in subtitles for anything spoken. Most people watch without sound.
