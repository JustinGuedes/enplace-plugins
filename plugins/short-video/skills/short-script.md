---
description: Write a 15 to 45 second script for a short video, shot by shot.
applies: writing a script for a TikTok, Reel or Short
---

# When to use
A piece of content is in its Script step and has a picked hook.

# Shape (about 30 seconds)
1. **Hook (0 to 2 s):** the picked hook, word for word, with its first shot.
2. **Show it (2 to 20 s):** three to five quick beats that prove the hook. One idea per beat, a new shot every two to three seconds.
3. **The turn (20 to 27 s):** the payoff, the reveal or the funny moment. This is what people share.
4. **The ask (27 to 30 s):** one line on where to get it, like "Free on the App Store". One ask, never three.

# How to write it
- Write it as a table: time, what we see, what we hear (voice, sound, text on screen).
- Real hands and real faces beat screen recordings. Use the app's screen only up close and only for a beat.
- Text on screen repeats the key words, for people watching muted.
- Name the sound: trending audio, a voice-over, or the game's own sounds.
- Note anything that has to be filmed so the person can record in one go.

# Check
- Could someone film it from this alone, today, with a phone?
- Does every beat earn its seconds? Cut the weakest one.
