---
description: Write the first two seconds of a short video so people stop scrolling.
applies: writing a hook, an opening line or the first shot of a short video
---

# When to use
Any short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) before the script is written. The hook decides whether anyone sees the rest.

# How
1. Write three hooks, each a different kind, so the person can pick:
   - **The stakes:** what's about to go wrong or right. "You have 10 seconds and your friend has the manual."
   - **The surprise:** something people don't expect to see. "We made a game where your friends can blow you up."
   - **The point of view:** put the viewer in the moment. "POV: you cut the wrong wire."
2. Keep each under 12 words, said in under two seconds.
3. Show it as well as say it: name the first shot beside each hook (hands on the phone, a face reacting, the screen up close).
4. Promise only what the video delivers. A hook that lies loses people at second three.
5. Read the project's voice notes in memory first and write in that voice.

# Avoid
- Starting with a greeting, a logo or "In this video".
- Questions people can answer "no" to.
- Hooks that need the caption to make sense.
