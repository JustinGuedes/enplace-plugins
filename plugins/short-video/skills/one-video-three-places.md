# When to use
A piece posts to more than one channel (its Post to field lists several).

# How
1. Edit once, in 9:16 at 1080×1920, under 60 seconds. Keep text and faces inside the middle 80%: each app covers the edges with its own buttons.
2. Export without any app's watermark. A TikTok watermark on Reels or Shorts gets it shown to fewer people.
3. Post TikTok first, then Reels and Shorts within a day, with each channel's caption (see captions-and-hashtags).
4. Use each app's own sound picker for trending audio; otherwise keep the original sound.
5. After each post, paste its link on the piece in Enplace. When every channel has its link, the piece moves to Posted.

# After two days
Note views and comments per channel on the piece. If one channel did far better, say so in the project's memory as what works.
