# When to use
Submitting an app or an update for App Store or Play review.

# How
1. Say in two sentences what the app does and what changed in this version.
2. If anything needs signing in, give a demo account that works and has sample data.
3. Explain anything a reviewer could mistake for a problem: purchases, permissions, content behind a paywall, features that need hardware.
4. Name where to find the new feature: the tab and the button.
5. Keep secrets out of the notes beyond the demo account.

# Check before handing over
- The demo account was tried today.
- The notes match the build being submitted.
