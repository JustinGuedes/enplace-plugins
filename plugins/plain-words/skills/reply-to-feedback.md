description: Answer a review, an email or a bug report kindly and briefly.
applies: replying to a customer, a reviewer or a tester
---

# When to use
Replying to an App Store review, a support email, a bug report or a tester's note.

# How
1. Thank them for one specific thing they said.
2. Answer the actual question or problem first. If it's fixed, say in which version.
3. If you need something from them, ask for one thing, plainly.
4. No blame, no excuses, no promises with dates you don't control.
5. Three short sentences is usually enough.

# Check before sending
- Is the person's name and the product's name right?
- Hand the draft to the person to send; don't send it yourself.
