description: Turn a list of changes into notes people who use the product care about.
applies: writing release notes or What's new
---

# When to use
Writing release notes, a "What's new" or a changelog entry that people who use the product will read.

# How
1. List the changes, then cut anything a user wouldn't notice.
2. Lead with the change people will like most.
3. Say what they can do now, not what you did: "Restore purchases from Settings" rather than "Fixed restore flow".
4. One line each, at most four lines in the store's text.
5. Leave out ticket keys, code names and internal tools.

# Check before handing over
- Would someone who has never seen the code understand every line?
- Does it match the product's voice (see memory)?
