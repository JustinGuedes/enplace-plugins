---
description: Write a status update someone can read in ten seconds.
applies: writing an update, a report or a summary
---

# When to use
Any update, report or summary for a person: a work item's report, a weekly note, a message to a collaborator.

# How
1. Open with the outcome or the ask, in one sentence. "The paywall fix is live" beats "I've been working on the paywall".
2. Then what it depends on, if anything: a decision, a merge, a date.
3. Keep it to what changed since the last update. Don't replay the work.
4. Full sentences, short words. No arrows, no fragments, no jargon the reader didn't use first.
5. One follow-up at most, said once.

# Check before sending
- Could they act on it after reading only the first line?
- Is every claim backed by a link or something you checked?
